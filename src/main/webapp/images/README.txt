12 company logo-style SVG placeholders for the JobPortal project. These are custom illustrative wordmark-style placeholders, not official company logo artwork. Copy all .svg files into src/main/webapp/images (or your project's webapp/images folder). Run update_company_logos.sql after confirming all 12 company rows exist in the companies table. The JSP image path should be ${pageContext.request.contextPath}/images/${job.logoImage} or use the matching company bean property.
